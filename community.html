You submitted. Now what?

Turn one data point into community action.

Submitting your numbers is useful. Sharing the project and showing up prepared is what makes the information matter.

Share this with your neighbors

Ask other Maple Plain property owners to submit their assessment so the dataset becomes more representative.

Contact the people who can respond

Use the contact list for the assessor, Town officials, Wisconsin DOR, legislators, and the Governor's constituent-services office. Keep a record of each attempt and response.

Do your homework

Gather your notice, property record card, factual property details, recent comparable sales, photographs, and your own opinion of market value.

Attend Open Book

Friday, October 23, noon to 2 p.m. Ask what sales and adjustments support your value and document the answers.

Protect your Board of Review rights

If the issue is not resolved, follow the notice and objection deadlines. Do not assume an unanswered message extends your filing deadline.

Spread the word

More submissions make the patterns more useful.

Share the project on Facebook, text the link to lake neighbors, or bring it to a neighborhood conversation.

Community notes

Free-text comments are not automatically published.

Resident comments may contain personal information, mistakes, or allegations that have not been verified. We keep those private by default. If community themes or representative comments are published later, they will be reviewed first and stripped of identifying information.
