Why this project exists

Turn individual frustration into organized evidence.

A dramatic increase does not automatically prove an assessment is wrong. What matters is whether the assessed value reflects the property's market value under Wisconsin law and whether the underlying property facts and comparable sales actually support that value.

This project is designed to help residents ask better questions, preserve deadlines, share non-identifying community data, and arrive at Open Book and Board of Review prepared.

Facts travel farther than outrage. Keep copies of notices, record contact attempts, request the assessor's supporting records, and build a clear timeline.
