Deadlines matter

Know the dates before you do anything else.

Wisconsin's assessment appeal process has procedural deadlines. Protect those rights even if you are still waiting for an answer from the assessor.

Do not assume a phone call or unanswered email extends a deadline.

The dates that matter right now

The Town of Maple Plain currently posts the following Open Book and Board of Review schedule. Always confirm with the Town website or Clerk in case anything changes.

Open Book
Friday, Oct. 23

Noon to 2:00 p.m.

Informal review with the assessor. Ask for the property record card, comparable sales, sales analysis, and explanation of the valuation.

Notice of intent to object
At least 48 hours before BOR

For a Nov. 7 meeting at 10:00 a.m., the 48-hour point is Thursday, Nov. 5 at 10:00 a.m.

Earlier is safer. Confirm receipt with the Board of Review clerk.

Board of Review
Saturday, Nov. 7

10:00 a.m. to noon

This is the formal appeal stage. A completed PA-115A objection form and evidence supporting your requested value are critical.

Important: Wisconsin DOR says the owner must give the Board of Review clerk written or oral notice of intent to object at least 48 hours before the first scheduled BOR meeting. The written PA-115A objection is considered filed if the clerk receives it within the first two hours of the first BOR meeting. Do not wait for the last possible moment.

A simple timeline

What to do between now and Board of Review

Now: verify your property facts

Compare acreage, frontage, square footage, building characteristics, utilities, septic, seasonal use, access, and condition against the assessor's property record.

Before Open Book: gather questions and comparables

Bring your old and new assessment numbers, property photos, any recent appraisal or sale information, and a written list of questions.

At Open Book: ask the assessor to show the work

Ask which sales were used, how differences were adjusted, and what methodology produced the land and improvement values.

Before the BOR deadline: protect your appeal rights

Give notice of intent to object, complete the PA-115A, and keep proof that the Clerk received your materials.

Before your hearing: organize your evidence

Lead with your requested value and the strongest evidence supporting it. Bring copies and a clean timeline of contacts and records requested.
