Evidence beats outrage

Build a packet that makes your argument easy to understand.

The Board of Review needs evidence supporting your requested value. Your job is to make the property facts, market evidence, and valuation differences clear enough that a stranger can follow them quickly.

Do this today: start one folder for every assessment notice, photo, comparable sale, email, voicemail note, and official form.

Core packet

What to gather

Do not wait until the night before the hearing. Build this as information arrives.

1. Assessment documents

  • 2026 changed-assessment notice
  • Prior assessment notice or tax bill
  • Parcel number and legal description
  • Assessor's property record card
  • Completed PA-115A objection form
  • Proof of notice and delivery to the Clerk

2. Property facts

  • Lot acreage and usable area
  • Water frontage and shoreline characteristics
  • Slope, access, wetlands, easements, or restrictions
  • Building square footage, age, and condition
  • Running water, septic, heat, and other utilities
  • Seasonal versus year-round use

3. Market evidence

  • Recent arm's-length sale of your property, if any
  • Recent genuinely comparable property sales
  • Professional appraisal, if available
  • Photos showing meaningful property differences
  • Written adjustments explaining why a comp is higher or lower
  • Your own requested market value and how you reached it
Lake property needs context

A nearby property is not automatically a good comparable.

For lakeshore property, two parcels on the same road can have very different market characteristics. Compare the things a buyer would actually care about.

  • Same lake or a meaningfully similar water body
  • Water frontage and shoreline quality
  • Lot size and usable/buildable area
  • Slope to the water and ease of access
  • Road access and seasonal accessibility
  • Running water, well, septic, electric, heat
  • Condition, age, size, and quality of structures
  • View, privacy, restrictions, easements, wetlands
The useful question is not “Is it close by?” It is “Would a typical buyer see these properties as substitutes, and how should the differences affect price?”

Contact log

Document every request and response.

If information is delayed or unavailable, a clean timeline is stronger than trying to reconstruct frustrating conversations later.

Record the date

Write down when you called, emailed, left voicemail, mailed something, or spoke in person.

Record what you requested

Property record card, sales analysis, comparable sales, methodology, filing confirmation, or hearing information.

Record the response

Who responded, when, what was provided, and what remains outstanding. Save screenshots and emails.
