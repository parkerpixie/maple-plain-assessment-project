Why share?

One unusual assessment is a story. Dozens of structured submissions reveal a pattern.

Aggregated community data can help residents understand whether increases differ by lakefront status, property type, land versus improvements, utilities, and other characteristics. Exact addresses, names, emails, phone numbers, and parcel numbers will not be shown in a public dataset.
