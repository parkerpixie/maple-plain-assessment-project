Use official channels

Contact the people who can answer questions, receive filings, and escalate concerns.

Be factual, specific, and persistent without flooding one office with duplicate messages. Save every response and keep your appeal deadlines moving even while you wait.

What should I say?

Keep the message simple and specific.

Sample message:

I am a Town of Maple Plain property owner concerned about the 2026 reassessment. My property's assessed value changed from $_____ to $_____, a change of _____%. I am asking your office to review the concerns being raised by affected property owners and help ensure residents receive clear information about the valuation methodology, comparable sales, appeal process, and deadlines. Please confirm receipt and let me know what action your office can take.

Add one specific request. For example: “Please send me my property record card and the comparable sales used for this assessment,” or “Please confirm that my notice of intent to object has been received.”
